# Voter factors research — 2026-10-11

## Modules shipped

1. **Coalition & red lines** (`coalition.json`) — all 17 parties
2. **MMP viability notes** (`viability.json`) — all 17 parties
3. **Track-record compare** UI over existing `track-record.json`

## Method

- Majors: RNZ / 1News / Spinoff 2026 rule-in/out coverage + Opportunity official bottom lines + 2023 National-ACT / National-NZ First agreements (S93, S109)
- Minors: party site citations; `unknown` when no clear preference published
- Viability: list lengths from Electoral Commission lists PDF (S04) + MMP help (S05/S138); electorate-focus flagged for Te Pati Maori and Te Tai Tokerau from short lists / messaging

## Honesty gaps

- Many minor parties have no published coalition matrix — correctly `unknown`
- Bottom lines are campaign claims, not guarantees of post-election behaviour
- Threshold notes are mechanical MMP facts, not “don’t vote for X”
