### Kiwi Tax Plan income-tax scale
- **Instrument:** income_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 0% to $30k; 15%/33%/39%/42%; 48% above $300,001
- **Summary:** Zero tax on first $30,000; progressive bands to 48% above $300,001; party says most people pay less income tax.
- **Sources:** S53, S50
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Tiered net wealth tax above $2m
- **Instrument:** wealth_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 0% to $2m; 1.5% / 2% / 2.5% above bands
- **Summary:** Progressive annual tax on net wealth above $2m per person.
- **Sources:** S53, S50, S37
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### 5% stamp duty on residential sales
- **Instrument:** other_levy
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 5%; FHB exemption under $1m
- **Summary:** 5% on residential sales; first-home buyers exempt for homes under $1m (buyer-side transaction tax, distinct from Labour CGT).
- **Sources:** S50, S53
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### 33% land-banking tax + 2% vacant house tax
- **Instrument:** land_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 33% land-banking; 2% vacant house
- **Summary:** Kiwi Tax Plan: 33% land-banking tax and 2% annual vacant house tax.
- **Sources:** S53
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Company tax to 33%
- **Instrument:** company_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 33%
- **Summary:** Return headline company rate from 28% to 33%.
- **Sources:** S53, S51
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Kai credit / No GST on kai framing
- **Instrument:** gst
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** Credit for earners <= $60k; GST-off-food bill separate
- **Summary:** Targeted credit for earners at or under $60,000 framed as equivalent to weeks of kai; separate members' bill seeks GST removal from food.
- **Sources:** S53, S84
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### 5% international profit transfer tax
- **Instrument:** other_levy
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 5%
- **Summary:** 5% on value of profits transferred offshore.
- **Sources:** S53
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

## Coalition

- **Stance:** selective
- **Summary:** Te Pati Maori runs an electorate-centred strategy with a one-person list and has published Te Tiriti constitutional demands as negotiation bottom lines; it seeks a left-leaning change of government while Labour has left the door ajar but rejected some TPM proposals.
- **Prefers:** Change-of-government path with Labour/Greens; campaign messaging has urged party votes for Labour or Greens while focusing on Maori electorates.
- **Rules out:** Opposes the current governing coalition's Treaty and Maori policy agenda.
- **Bottom lines:** Te Tiriti entrenchment / constitutional pathway: independent Te Tiriti commission powers, binding Waitangi Tribunal recommendations, and related funding proposals (Labour has rejected key elements).
- **Sources:** S137, S141, S142, S135
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11
