### Fair income tax restructure
- **Instrument:** income_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 0% to $9,999; then 10%/17.5%/25.5%/30.5%/33.5%; 45% from $160,000
- **Summary:** Tax-free first $10,000 and stepped bands up to 45% over $160,000; party says 96% get a tax cut. Document also appeared online before the launch press conference.
- **Sources:** S76, S31, S75
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### 2.5% wealth tax on net assets above $10m
- **Instrument:** wealth_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 2.5% p.a. above $10m net (individual)
- **Summary:** Annual tax on net assets above $10m per individual ($20m couple framing in some coverage); family home exempt; trust attribution rules.
- **Sources:** S76, S31
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Capital Acquisitions Tax 33%
- **Instrument:** other_levy
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 33% above $1m received
- **Summary:** 33% on assets/gifts received over $1m; family home and family farms exempt.
- **Sources:** S76, S31
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### 33% company tax for large corporates
- **Instrument:** company_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 33% if turnover > $30m; else 28%
- **Summary:** 33% for firms with turnover over $30m; keep 28% for SMEs.
- **Sources:** S76, S31
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Bank levy 0.06%
- **Instrument:** other_levy
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 0.06% on big-bank liabilities
- **Summary:** 0.06% p.a. on liabilities of banks with over $100b liabilities (big four).
- **Sources:** S76, S31
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### 10-year bright-line + reverse interest deductibility
- **Instrument:** capital_gains
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 10-year bright-line; marginal rates on taxed gain
- **Summary:** Restore 10-year bright-line on residential investment; remove interest deductibility for residential investors; gains taxed at marginal rates under bright-line (not Labour's flat 28% CGT).
- **Sources:** S76, S31
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Big tech offshore profit withholding
- **Instrument:** other_levy
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 5% withholding
- **Summary:** Enforce 5% withholding on profits shifted offshore via misclassified royalties/service fees.
- **Sources:** S76
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### green-2020-clean-car
- **Window:** 2020-2023
- **Status:** delivered (support)
- **Pledge:** As confidence-and-supply partner, advance Zero Carbon Act goals including clean vehicle uptake measures.
- **Outcome:** Clean Car Discount / clean vehicle settings were introduced in 2021-22 under the Labour-Greens support arrangement; later repealed after 2023.
- **Sources:** S106, S94
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### green-2020-ag-emissions
- **Window:** 2020-2023
- **Status:** partial (support)
- **Pledge:** Price agricultural greenhouse gas emissions (farm-level system targeted by mid-2020s via He Waka Eke Noa pathway).
- **Outcome:** Policy design and Cabinet work progressed, but farm-level pricing was not operating by the 2023 election and was later paused/reversed by the new government.
- **Sources:** S107, S108
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### green-2020-wealth-tax
- **Window:** 2020-2023
- **Status:** blocked_coalition (support)
- **Pledge:** Campaign for a wealth tax; seek progress while supporting a Labour government.
- **Outcome:** Labour ruled out a wealth tax for the term; no wealth tax was enacted.
- **Sources:** S100, S101
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### green-2023-oppose-treaty-principles
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (opposition)
- **Pledge:** Oppose the Treaty Principles Bill and mobilise against it.
- **Outcome:** Greens opposed the bill alongside Labour and Te Pati Maori; it was defeated at second reading in April 2025.
- **Sources:** S104, S126
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### green-2023-oppose-smokefree-repeal
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** not_delivered (opposition)
- **Pledge:** Oppose repeal of the 2022 smoked-tobacco endgame measures.
- **Outcome:** Greens voted against repeal; the Smokefree Environments and Regulated Products Amendment Act 2024 still removed the generation ban, retail cut and denicotinisation settings.
- **Sources:** S120, S121
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### green-2017-zero-carbon
- **Window:** 2017-2020
- **Status:** delivered (support)
- **Pledge:** Confidence-and-supply: introduce a Zero Carbon Act and independent Climate Commission.
- **Outcome:** Climate Change Response (Zero Carbon) Amendment Act received royal assent in November 2019, establishing the Climate Change Commission.
- **Sources:** S204, S205
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### green-2017-welfare-overhaul
- **Window:** 2017-2020
- **Status:** partial (support)
- **Pledge:** C&S agreement: overhaul welfare — improve access, review sanctions, and lift incomes.
- **Outcome:** Welfare Expert Advisory Group reported in 2019; some benefit and sanction changes followed, but a full overhaul was not completed by 2020.
- **Sources:** S206, S207
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### green-2017-cannabis-referendum
- **Window:** 2017-2020
- **Status:** not_delivered (support)
- **Pledge:** C&S agreement: hold a referendum on legalising personal-use cannabis by the 2020 election.
- **Outcome:** 2020 referendum held; official result was No — Cannabis Legalisation and Control Bill not enacted.
- **Sources:** S208, S209
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### green-2008-2017-asset-sales-referendum
- **Window:** 2008-2017
- **Status:** partial (opposition)
- **Pledge:** As opposition, support a citizens-initiated referendum against partial asset sales.
- **Outcome:** 2013 referendum returned a large No majority, but the National government continued remaining sales.
- **Sources:** S189, S210
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

## Coalition

- **Stance:** selective
- **Summary:** The Greens seek to replace the current government alongside Labour and push progressive tax and climate priorities; published 2026 materials emphasise policy ambition more than a fixed multi-party rule-out list.
- **Prefers:** Positions for a change of government with Labour; historically provided confidence and supply / cooperation.
- **Rules out:** Opposes continuing the National-ACT-NZ First government agenda (campaign opposition posture).
- **Bottom lines:** Major tax and redistribution proposals (e.g. wealth tax framing) remain core platform asks in negotiations with Labour, though Labour has refused to adopt them.
- **Sources:** S136, S75, S16
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11
