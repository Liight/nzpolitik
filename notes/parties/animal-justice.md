### GST off plant foods and veterinary services
- **Instrument:** gst
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 0% on listed plant foods and vet services
- **Summary:** Zero-rate GST on fruit, vegetables, legumes, nuts; remove GST on all veterinary services.
- **Sources:** S43, S63
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Split ETS + biogenic scheme + pollution tax
- **Instrument:** carbon_ets
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** Pollution tax rate not specified
- **Summary:** Fossil ETS separate from biogenic cap-and-trade; pollution tax on pesticides/fertilisers escalating over time; revenue to farm transition fund.
