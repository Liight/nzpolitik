### Tax Reset income scale + Citizen's Income
- **Instrument:** income_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 28% to $50k; 34% $50k-$200k; 39% above $200k; CI about $19,400/yr
- **Summary:** Three personal brackets plus universal Citizen's Income for adults 18+; replaces most main benefits with top-ups (Tax Reset PDF).
- **Sources:** S54, S33
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### National land value tax
- **Instrument:** land_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 1.75% urban; 0.5% rural (transition from 0.5% urban)
- **Summary:** Annual tax on unimproved land value to fund Citizen's Income; exemptions/deferrals for some public/Maori/social uses, retirees, farmers. Transition starts lower on urban land.
- **Sources:** S54, S33, S33a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### KiwiSaver 2.0 compulsory to 12%
- **Instrument:** kiwisaver_super
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 12% combined after glidepath
- **Summary:** Compulsory contributions rising to 6% employee + 6% employer; contributions and fund income progressively tax-exempt; NZ Super replaced by CI + top-up with no cut vs current Super.
- **Sources:** S54, S33
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Strengthen ETS; ring-fence auction revenue
- **Instrument:** carbon_ets
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** Revenue ring-fencing (no new ETS price stated)
- **Summary:** Restore credible rising ETS price path; direct Crown ETS auction revenue to Industry Energy Resilience Fund.
- **Sources:** S69
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### No CGT in Tax Reset (LVT preferred)
- **Instrument:** capital_gains
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** n/a — LVT preferred over CGT
- **Summary:** Tax Reset does not propose a capital gains tax; party favours land value tax plus universal income instead.
- **Sources:** S54
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

## Coalition

- **Stance:** open
- **Summary:** Opportunity published five formal 2026 bottom lines and says it will talk to either major party; National has ruled Opportunity out over tax policy while Opportunity says its door remains open.

- **Rules out:** National has ruled Opportunity out (external constraint); Opportunity still says its door to National remains open.
- **Bottom lines:** Empower Commerce Commission on anti-competitive market power (including structural separation tools). | Lobbying register/code and donation caps. | 10-year cross-party health plan with funding/workforce/infrastructure commitments. | Phase out bottom trawling (start with damaged inshore areas). | Recommit to Paris obligations and protect Climate Change Commission independence. | Will not support a government with Winston Peters as Foreign Minister (reported 2026 statement).
