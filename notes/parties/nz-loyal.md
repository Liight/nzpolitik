### 1% financial transaction tax replacing other taxes
- **Instrument:** other_levy
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 1% FTT
- **Summary:** 1% on electronic payments, cash withdrawals, online transactions, securities trades; framed as replacing income tax, GST, company tax and eliminating Inland Revenue.
- **Sources:** S61, S62, S39
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Abolish income tax (replaced by FTT)
- **Instrument:** income_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 0% (replaced by FTT)
- **Summary:** Conventional income tax eliminated under the 1% transaction tax model.
- **Sources:** S61
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11
