### Working for Families / tax credit reform
- **Instrument:** income_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** Abatement/indexation changes (no brackets)
- **Summary:** Supports folding In Work Tax Credit into Family Tax Credit; index WFF to wages; lower abatement rates — not a full income-tax scale.
- **Sources:** S67, S45
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### KiwiSaver equity for carers and women
- **Instrument:** kiwisaver_super
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** Contribution/support measures (not a contribution % schedule)
- **Summary:** State contributions to carers' KiwiSaver; employer KiwiSaver during maternity leave; pay equity framing.
- **Sources:** S68
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Living wages and benefits
- **Instrument:** other_levy
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** n/a — transfers/wages focus
- **Summary:** Increase minimum wages and benefits to living incomes; pay equity; affordable childcare.
