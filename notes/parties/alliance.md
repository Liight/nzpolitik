### First $20,000 tax-free + rebalanced scale
- **Instrument:** income_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 0% to $20k; then 17.5%/30%/34%/39%/47% bands (see costings PDF)
- **Summary:** Costed plan: tax-free first $20k; progressive rates up to a 47% top bracket; lower/middle earners pay less.
- **Sources:** S56, S57, S55
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### 2.5% wealth tax above $2m + trust measure
- **Instrument:** wealth_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 2.5% above $2m individual; trust tax ~1.5% (costings)
- **Summary:** Annual tax on individual net wealth above $2m (~$4m couple); companion trust tax closes avoidance.
- **Sources:** S56, S57
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### 33% CGT on realised gains (family home exempt)
- **Instrument:** capital_gains
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 33% on realised gains
- **Summary:** Broad CGT on investment property, shares, business assets; family home excluded; applies to gains after commencement.
- **Sources:** S56, S57
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Company tax to 30%
- **Instrument:** company_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 30%
- **Summary:** Increase company tax from 28% to 30% with behavioural adjustment in costings.
- **Sources:** S56, S57
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### 0.1% financial transactions levy
- **Instrument:** other_levy
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 0.1%
- **Summary:** Levy on equity and onshore FX turnover; wages, bills, KiwiSaver contributions exempt.
- **Sources:** S56, S57
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Replace ETS with carbon tax
- **Instrument:** carbon_ets
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** Carbon tax rate not specified in PDF
- **Summary:** Scrap ETS for a direct carbon tax; revenue to renewables/PT/efficiency; protect low-income households. Rate not specified in environment PDF.
- **Sources:** S58
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### $10,000 KiwiSaver endowment; Super at 65
- **Instrument:** kiwisaver_super
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** $10,000 endowment
- **Summary:** Keep NZ Super at 65; $10,000 automatic endowment to new KiwiSaver accounts; couple rate lift toward 72.5% of net average wage cap.
- **Sources:** S34
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

## Issue positions

### Economy & tax
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Costed plan: tax-free $20k, wealth tax 2.5% above $2m, 33% CGT, 30% company tax, 0.1% FTT, carbon tax replacing ETS — see tax dossier.
- **Sources:** S11a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Health
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Free healthcare in every community including GP, dental, optical and audiology via a public Community Health Service.
- **Sources:** S11a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Housing
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Five-year public housing programme; Kainga Ora rents and state-backed mortgage repayments capped at 25% of net household income; co-ownership pathways.
- **Sources:** S11a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Climate & environment
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Public ownership of electricity and water, a carbon tax, and a just transition supporting workers and communities.
- **Sources:** S11a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Education
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Platform calls for free education from primary through tertiary level.
- **Sources:** S11a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Justice & crime

- **Verified:** 2026-10-11
