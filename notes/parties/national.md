### No new taxes pledge
- **Instrument:** other_levy
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** No new tax instruments
- **Summary:** Campaign promise of no new taxes; ruled out bank tax and accommodation/bed levy. Has not ruled out raising existing taxes/fees.
- **Sources:** S77, S32
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### No capital gains tax
- **Instrument:** capital_gains
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** No CGT
- **Summary:** Commits to no CGT under the no-new-taxes frame and campaigns against Labour's property CGT.
- **Sources:** S77
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### No wealth tax
- **Instrument:** wealth_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** No wealth tax
- **Summary:** Framed as part of no-new-taxes versus partners' wealth taxes.
- **Sources:** S77
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Compulsory KiwiSaver to 12% by 2032
- **Instrument:** kiwisaver_super
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 6%+6% by 2032; compulsory from 1 July 2028
- **Summary:** Compulsory for workers from 1 July 2028; default employee/employer rates rise to 6% each by 1 April 2032; Baby Boost $1,500; parental leave and over-65 employer contributions.
- **Sources:** S79, S32a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Student loan repayment rate cut to 10%
- **Instrument:** other_levy
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 10% above $24,128 from 1 April 2027
- **Summary:** Reduce compulsory repayment from 12% to 10% on income above $24,128 from 1 April 2027 for NZ-based borrowers; tougher overseas recovery.
- **Sources:** S78
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Bracket creep / income-tax adjustment signalled
- **Instrument:** income_tax
- **Status:** reported
- **Rate/threshold:** No rates/thresholds published yet
- **Summary:** Willis said National will campaign on addressing bracket creep and make an income-tax announcement during the campaign, but not before books return to surplus. No rates published as of 2026-10-11.
- **Sources:** S80
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

## Issue positions

### Economy & tax
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** No new taxes; no CGT/wealth tax; compulsory KiwiSaver to 12%; student-loan repay 10%; bracket-creep cut signalled but uncosted — see tax dossier.
- **Sources:** S18a, S18b
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Health
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** More medicines and earlier care; more of health spend focused on serious illness treatment at home.
- **Sources:** S18a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Housing
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Expand access to 5% first-home deposits via First Home Loan; easier commercial building consents; energy fund for home electrification.
- **Sources:** S18a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Climate & environment
- **Stance:** mixed
- **Summary:** Nine-point affordable/reliable power plan; enabling rules for wind, solar and hydro in RMA replacement; hunting/fishing and fisheries plans alongside growth framing.
- **Sources:** S18a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Education
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Screen-use limits including ending screens in ECE and limits in primary; graduate student-loan repayment changes.
- **Sources:** S18a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Justice & crime
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Tougher sentences for sexual offending (abolish good-character assessments at sentencing for sexual offending); modernise sexual consent to affirmative yes; more police on Auckland buses/trains.
- **Sources:** S18a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Immigration
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Skilled residence pathways and stronger compliance/deportation settings; visitor levy and Trans-Tasman travel items on 2026 policies index.
- **Sources:** S18c, S18a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Te Tiriti / Maori issues
- **Stance:** mixed
- **Summary:** Uphold settlement delivery and equal citizenship principle; oppose broad co-governance expansions in party messaging.
- **Sources:** S18d
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Foreign policy & defence
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Trade/export growth (Next Billion Customers); closer Australia travel cooperation.
- **Sources:** S18a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Social issues
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Modernise paid parental leave; compulsory KiwiSaver enhancements; firm/fair welfare focused on employment; affirmative consent law modernisation.
- **Sources:** S18a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2023-tax-relief
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Campaign Back Pocket Boost: raise income-tax thresholds and deliver middle-income tax relief.
- **Outcome:** Budget 2024 raised personal thresholds from 31 July 2024 and expanded related credits, largely matching the campaign package with coalition funding adjustments.
- **Sources:** S87, S88
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2023-three-waters-repeal
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** partial (governing)
- **Pledge:** Repeal Labour Three Waters / Affordable Water reforms.
- **Outcome:** Water Services Acts Repeal Act 2024 repealed the entity model in February 2024; Local Water Done Well replacement legislation continued to roll out afterward.
- **Sources:** S89, S90
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2023-bright-line
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Reduce the residential bright-line test from 10 years to 2 years.
- **Outcome:** Legislation reinstated a 2-year bright-line test applying to disposals with a bright-line end date on or after 1 July 2024.
- **Sources:** S91, S92
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2023-foreign-buyers-tax
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** blocked_coalition (governing)
- **Pledge:** Fund part of tax relief via a foreign residential buyers tax while keeping the foreign-buyer ban.
- **Outcome:** National-NZ First coalition agreement stopped the foreign buyers tax; tax relief was funded through other savings and revenue measures instead.
- **Sources:** S93, S87
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2023-clean-car-discount
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** End the Clean Car Discount (feebate) scheme.
- **Outcome:** Land Transport (Clean Vehicle Discount Scheme Repeal) Amendment Act 2023 repealed the scheme in the government's early agenda.
- **Sources:** S94, S95
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2020-oppose-light-rail
- **Window:** 2020-2023
- **Status:** partial (opposition)
- **Pledge:** As opposition, oppose Labour Auckland light rail delivery failures and argue the project should not proceed as promised.
- **Outcome:** Auckland light rail was not built in the 2020-23 term; the competing-bid process stalled and construction never started before the 2023 election.
- **Sources:** S96, S97
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2023-cancel-light-rail
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Cancel Auckland Light Rail and reprioritise Auckland transport investment.
- **Outcome:** Cabinet cancelled the Auckland Light Rail project in December 2023; the delivery company was wound up and no track was built under that project.
- **Sources:** S116, S117
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2008-tax-cuts
- **Window:** 2008-2017
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** 2008 campaign: deliver National personal income-tax cuts and related threshold changes early in a National-led government.
- **Outcome:** Taxation (Urgent Measures and Annual Rates) legislation in December 2008 implemented National's income-tax package.
- **Sources:** S159, S160
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2010-gst-tax-switch
- **Window:** 2008-2017
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Budget 2010 tax switch: raise GST to 15% from 1 October 2010 and cut personal income-tax rates, with compensation.
- **Outcome:** GST rose to 15% and personal tax rates were reduced from 1 October 2010 with compensation adjustments.
- **Sources:** S161, S162
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2008-90-day-trials
- **Window:** 2008-2017
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Introduce 90-day trial periods for new employees as a pre-election employment-relations commitment.
- **Outcome:** Employment Relations Amendment Act 2008 created statutory 90-day trial provisions from 1 March 2009 for smaller employers.
- **Sources:** S163, S164
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2008-ufb
- **Window:** 2008-2017
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** 2008 election: invest Crown capital to accelerate ultra-fast broadband fibre rollout for most New Zealanders.
- **Outcome:** Government finalised the UFB initiative, signed Crown Fibre partner deals, and began physical rollout from 2010.
- **Sources:** S165, S166
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2011-mixed-ownership
- **Window:** 2008-2017
- **Status:** partial (governing)
- **Pledge:** 2011 election: extend mixed ownership — sell up to 49% of selected energy SOEs while keeping majority Crown control.
- **Outcome:** MOM legislation passed; IPOs proceeded for Mighty River, Meridian and Genesis; Solid Energy never reached IPO (partial programme).
- **Sources:** S167, S168
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2017-2020-oppose-cgt
- **Window:** 2017-2020
- **Status:** delivered (opposition)
- **Pledge:** As opposition, oppose a broad capital gains tax and commit that National would not introduce one.
- **Outcome:** In April 2019 the Labour-led government abandoned CGT for lack of coalition consensus; no comprehensive CGT was enacted that term.
- **Sources:** S169, S170
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### national-2017-2020-oil-gas-ban-reverse
- **Window:** 2017-2020
- **Status:** not_delivered (opposition)
- **Pledge:** Pledge to reverse Labour's ban on new offshore oil and gas exploration permits if returned in 2020.
- **Outcome:** National did not win 2020; Crown Minerals (Petroleum) Amendment Act 2018 kept the offshore ban through this window.
- **Sources:** S171, S172
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

## Coalition

- **Stance:** selective
- **Summary:** National anchors the centre-right bloc with ACT and NZ First, has ruled out Opportunity and a grand coalition with Labour, and campaigns for a strong National party vote while publishing no-new-taxes as a coalition condition.
- **Prefers:** Continue governing arrangements with ACT and NZ First if numbers allow (2023 coalition partners).
- **Rules out:** Ruled out Opportunity (tax/spend criteria confirmed by PMO). | Ruled out a National-Labour grand coalition. | Campaign framing excludes Labour, Greens, and Te Pati Maori as governing partners.
- **Bottom lines:** No new taxes to be solidified in any renewed coalition agreement (2026 campaign). | Partner test: will not do business with parties that want to spend more, tax more, borrow more.
- **Sources:** S133, S134, S146, S109
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11
