- **Instrument:** kiwisaver_super
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 0% tax on qualifying KiwiSaver earnings
- **Summary:** Tax-free qualifying fund earnings; end government KiwiSaver contribution for members with employer contributions; party PDF costings ~$2.4b over four years.

- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

## Issue positions

### Economy & tax
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** No tax on KiwiSaver earnings; scrap net-zero / ETS price floor with carbon-tax refund; oppose CGT/wealth taxes — see tax dossier.
- **Sources:** S10a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Health
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Supports a competitive public-private health mix, closing the medicines gap with Australia, pharmacy-led care, measurable targets, and strengthening End of Life Choice; notes abolition of the Maori Health Authority while in government.
- **Sources:** S10b
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Housing
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Property-rights planning reform, faster building, council incentives to consent homes, and social-housing changes emphasising contribution and time-limited tenancies.
- **Sources:** S10c
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Climate & environment
- **Stance:** opposes
- **Summary:** Economy policy campaign includes scrapping net-zero framing and cutting carbon-tax costs.
- **Sources:** S10a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Education
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Education policy emphasises standards, charter schools, affordable uniforms, rural bus access and equal treatment at universities.
- **Sources:** S10d
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Justice & crime
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Law and order policy: tougher consequences, three-strikes expansion, deportation of serious offenders, rural and youth crime measures.
- **Sources:** S10e
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Immigration
- **Stance:** mixed
- **Summary:** Infrastructure policy requires temporary workers to contribute to infrastructure from day one; no full immigration hub published.
- **Sources:** S10c
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Te Tiriti / Maori issues
- **Stance:** opposes
- **Summary:** Proposes defining Treaty principles via binding referendum; equal rights before the law and opposition to co-governance expansion.
- **Sources:** S10f, S10
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Foreign policy & defence
- **Stance:** unknown
- **Summary:** No clear published position found on this issue in materials checked as of 2026-10-11 (act.org.nz).
- **Sources:** S10
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Social issues
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Equal rights and democracy is a named policy pillar alongside personal responsibility messaging.
- **Sources:** S10
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### act-2023-treaty-principles
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** partial (governing)
- **Pledge:** Introduce a Treaty Principles Bill based on ACT policy (coalition: support to select committee).
- **Outcome:** Bill was introduced and supported to select committee; National and NZ First opposed at second reading and it was defeated 112-11 in April 2025.
- **Sources:** S109, S126
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### act-2023-three-strikes
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Restore Three Strikes sentencing legislation (National-ACT coalition commitment).
- **Outcome:** Sentencing (Reinstating Three Strikes) Amendment Act 2024 passed; regime in force from 17 June 2025.
- **Sources:** S109, S110
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### act-2023-flatten-tax
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** blocked_coalition (governing)
- **Pledge:** Flatten / simplify the personal income-tax scale.
- **Outcome:** Finance Minister engaged ACT on flattening for Budget 2024 but did not implement it; National-shaped threshold relief proceeded instead.
- **Sources:** S87, S88
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### act-2023-clean-car
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Repeal the Clean Car Discount (National-ACT coalition item).
- **Outcome:** Scheme repealed by Land Transport (Clean Vehicle Discount Scheme Repeal) Amendment Act 2023.
- **Sources:** S94, S95
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### act-2020-oppose-three-waters
- **Window:** 2020-2023
- **Status:** not_delivered (opposition)
- **Pledge:** As opposition, oppose Three Waters co-governance and centralisation reforms.
- **Outcome:** Three Waters / Affordable Water legislation still passed under Labour; ACT's opposition did not stop enactment in that term.
- **Sources:** S89, S90
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### act-2023-charter-schools
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Reintroduce charter schools / kura hourua (National-ACT coalition commitment).
- **Outcome:** Education and Training Amendment Act 2024 restored a statutory charter-school framework.
- **Sources:** S123, S122
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### act-2023-smokefree-repeal
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Repeal 2022 smoked-tobacco endgame measures (coalition commitment).
- **Outcome:** Smokefree Environments and Regulated Products Amendment Act 2024 removed retail reduction, denicotinisation and generation-ban provisions.
- **Sources:** S109, S121
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### act-2017-2020-oppose-gun-laws
- **Window:** 2017-2020
- **Status:** not_delivered (opposition)
- **Pledge:** As opposition after the March 2019 Christchurch attacks, ACT argued for a more carefully scoped firearms response and against rushed reform without adequate select-committee process.
- **Outcome:** Arms (Prohibited Firearms, Magazines, and Parts) Amendment Act 2019 passed under urgency with broad support; ACT's preferred slower pathway was not adopted.
- **Sources:** S173, S174
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### act-2008-three-strikes
- **Window:** 2008-2017
- **Status:** delivered (support)
- **Pledge:** 2008 confidence-and-supply: enact ACT's three-strikes regime for repeat serious offenders.
- **Outcome:** National and ACT agreed a three-strikes model in the Sentencing and Parole Reform Act 2010.
- **Sources:** S175, S176
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### act-2011-charter-schools

### act-2008-spending-reviews

## Coalition

- **Rules out:** Campaign framing against Labour/Greens/Te Pati Maori forming government.
- **Bottom lines:** 2023 National-ACT agreement items remain a reference for ACT priorities (e.g. Treaty Principles process to select committee, regulatory reform).
