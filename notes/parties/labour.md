### 28% CGT on investment and commercial property profits
- **Instrument:** capital_gains
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 28% on gains after 1 July 2027; family home/farms exempt
- **Summary:** Official PDF after RNZ weekend leak: flat 28% on net gains after 1 July 2027 on sale of commercial/residential investment property (family home exempt). Revenue ring-fenced for health including three free GP visits.
- **Sources:** S70, S30, S85
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### CGT valuation-day details largely post-election
- **Instrument:** capital_gains
- **Status:** reported
- **Rate/threshold:** Valuation baseline 1 July 2027; details TBA
- **Summary:** Reported: valuation methodology and defaults largely deferred until after the election (Tax Working Group options).
- **Sources:** S30b
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Not campaigning on a wealth tax
- **Instrument:** wealth_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** n/a — not a 2026 Labour campaign wealth tax
- **Summary:** Labour says the only new tax it campaigns on is the property CGT; wealth tax left to partners in any coalition.
- **Sources:** S30, S70
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Raise GST registration threshold to $80,000
- **Instrument:** gst
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** $80,000 registration threshold from 1 July 2028
- **Summary:** Compulsory GST registration threshold raised from $60,000 to $80,000 from 1 July 2028 (not a cut in the 15% GST rate).
- **Sources:** S71
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Repeal Investment Boost incentive
- **Instrument:** company_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** Repeal Investment Boost (headline company rate unchanged)
- **Summary:** Fiscal plan books revenue from repealing National's Investment Boost (20% upfront deduction for qualifying new assets); not a change to the 28% headline company rate.
- **Sources:** S72
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Strengthen KiwiSaver (employer-led)
- **Instrument:** kiwisaver_super
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** Employer to 6% by 2032; compulsory from 1 July 2028
- **Summary:** Compulsory employer contributions from 1 July 2028 rising to 6% by 2032; default employee 4%; parental leave and over-65 employer contributions.
- **Sources:** S73, S17a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### ETS review / stronger carbon price (not a carbon tax)
- **Instrument:** carbon_ets
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** No $/tonne target stated
- **Summary:** Urgent ETS review aiming for a meaningful carbon price; pause new forestry ETS registrations during review; will not campaign on agricultural emissions pricing.
- **Sources:** S86
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Streaming services levy — denied
- **Instrument:** other_levy
- **Status:** denied
- **Rate/threshold:** n/a
- **Summary:** Hipkins ruled out a streamers levy; related member's bill removed from ballot — not election policy.
- **Sources:** S74
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Reverse tobacco excise cut
- **Instrument:** other_levy
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** See fiscal plan table for amounts
- **Summary:** Fiscal plan includes reversing National's tobacco tax cut as a revenue line.
- **Sources:** S72
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

## Issue positions

### Economy & tax
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Announced 28% property CGT (after leak), GST registration to $80k, Investment Boost repeal, stronger KiwiSaver; streaming levy denied — see tax dossier.

### Health
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Three free GP visits/year via Medicard, free prescriptions from July 2027, free cervical screening and maternity scans phased in, graduate nurse job guarantee, Family Doctor Loan Scheme.
- **Sources:** S17a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Housing
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** Start Work Now includes Kainga Ora building social housing again alongside school/hospital repairs and apprenticeships.
- **Sources:** S17a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Climate & environment
- **Stance:** supports
- **Summary:** SolarSaver programme for solar without upfront cost for homeowners, renters and communities.
- **Sources:** S17a
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### Education
- **Stance:** supports

- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### labour-2020-39-tax
- **Window:** 2020-2023
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Introduce a 39% top personal income-tax rate on income over $180,000.
- **Outcome:** Taxation (Income Tax Rate and Other Amendments) legislation applied the 39% rate from the 2021-22 income year.
- **Sources:** S98, S99
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### labour-2020-no-cgt-wealth
- **Window:** 2020-2023
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** 2020 campaign ruled out a capital gains tax and a wealth tax for that term.
- **Outcome:** No CGT or wealth tax was introduced 2020-23; revenue focus stayed on the 39% rate and other measures.

## Coalition

- **Stance:** selective
- **Summary:** Labour has hardened rule-outs against the incumbent bloc (especially NZ First and ACT), keeps Greens and Opportunity in play, leaves a narrower path with Te Pati Maori, and prefers not to adopt partners major tax packages beyond its own campaign commitments.
- **Prefers:** Continued working relationship with the Greens; door open to Opportunity. | Could work with Te Pati Maori; senior government roles for TPM described as unlikely. | Minority government with confidence-and-supply left on the table.
- **Rules out:** Will not work with parties in the current government (National, ACT, NZ First). | Official 2026 rule-out of NZ First (reported unanimous caucus). | Will not form a government with ACT.
- **Bottom lines:** Will not agree to abolish prisons or a Treaty Ombudsman with veto over Parliament (stated incompatibilities with some Te Pati Maori proposals). | Will not adopt Green wealth/corporate tax packages that contradict Labour's campaign tax commitments.
- **Sources:** S134, S135, S136, S139, S140
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11
