- **Instrument:** company_tax
- **Status:** announced
- **Rate/threshold:** 20% if turnover under $30m
- **Summary:** Cut company tax from 28% to 20% for companies with turnover under $30 million; party estimates ~$1b initial annual fiscal cost.

### nzf-2023-block-foreign-buyers-tax
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Coalition demand: progress National tax relief without a foreign residential buyers tax / without repealing the foreign-buyer ban for that purpose.
- **Outcome:** National-NZ First agreement excluded the foreign buyers tax; Budget 2024 funded relief via other measures.
- **Sources:** S93, S87
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### nzf-2023-smokefree-repeal
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Repeal 2022 smokefree smoked-tobacco amendments (denicotisation, retail cuts, generation ban) before March 2024.
- **Outcome:** Coalition progressed repeal of the Smokefree Environments smoked-tobacco amendments; associated Cabinet papers document delivery of the agreement.
- **Sources:** S93, S111
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### nzf-2023-fees-free
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Stop first-year Fees Free and replace with final-year Fees Free (no change before 2025).
- **Outcome:** Coalition agreements locked in switching Fees Free to the final year with the delayed start; implementation followed the agreement timetable.
- **Sources:** S93, S112
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### nzf-2023-treaty-principles-limit
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Support ACT Treaty Principles Bill only to select committee, not as enduring NZ First policy.
- **Outcome:** NZ First supported the bill to select committee then opposed progression; bill failed second reading in April 2025.
- **Sources:** S109, S105
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### nzf-2023-disestablish-mha
- **Window:** 2023-2026
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Support disestablishing Te Aka Whai Ora (Maori Health Authority) as part of the coalition health reset.
- **Outcome:** Pae Ora (Disestablishment of Maori Health Authority) Amendment Act 2024 disestablished the authority (effective 30 June 2024).
- **Sources:** S124, S114
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### nz-first-2017-pgf
- **Window:** 2017-2020
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Coalition agreement: Regional Development / Provincial Growth Fund of about $1 billion a year.
- **Outcome:** Cabinet established the PGF in late 2017; fund opened for applications in 2018 with multi-year funding committed.
- **Sources:** S199, S200
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### nz-first-2017-foreign-buyers
- **Window:** 2017-2020
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Coalition agreement: stop non-resident foreigners buying existing houses; strengthen Overseas Investment Act.
- **Outcome:** Overseas Investment Amendment Act 2018 introduced residential land screening from October 2018.
- **Sources:** S194, S195
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### nz-first-2017-minimum-wage-20
- **Window:** 2017-2020
- **Status:** delivered (governing)
- **Pledge:** Coalition agreement: progressively raise the adult minimum wage toward $20 per hour.
- **Outcome:** Adult minimum wage reached $20.00/hour from 1 April 2021 via Minimum Wage Order.
- **Sources:** S201, S202
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

### nz-first-2008-2017-buy-back-assets
- **Window:** 2008-2017
- **Status:** not_delivered (opposition)
- **Pledge:** As opposition, commit to buy back shares in privatised SOEs at no more than the original sale price.
- **Outcome:** Partial SOE sales completed under National; NZ First was not in government and did not buy back sold stakes before 2017.
- **Sources:** S203, S189
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11

## Coalition

- **Stance:** selective
- **Summary:** NZ First campaigns as a populist partner able to restrain or reshape National-led governments; 2023 coalition text is the clearest published record of its deal-breaker style, while 2026 rule-outs focus on blocking Labour.
- **Prefers:** Governing arrangements with National (2023 National-NZ First coalition).
- **Rules out:** Ruled out doing a deal with Labour for 2026 (repeated public statements).
- **Bottom lines:** 2023 agreement priorities (e.g. foreign buyers tax blocked; smokefree repeal; Fees Free switch) show NZ First uses coalition deals to hard-wire vetoes.
- **Sources:** S93, S134, S19, S139
- **Verified:** 2026-10-11
