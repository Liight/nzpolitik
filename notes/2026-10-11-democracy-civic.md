# Democracy civic page research (2026-10-11)

Cited timeline and why-vote copy for `/democracy`.

## Why vote

- Politics continues whether individuals vote; seats filled from ballots cast (vote.nz / Elections NZ MMP explainers S02, S05, S147).
- Worked example: 100 enrolled, 30 non-voters, 51% of ballots cast → ~35.7% of enrolled (`turnout.ts`). Illustrative only.
